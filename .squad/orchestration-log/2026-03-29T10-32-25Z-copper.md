## Summary

Copper reviewed `src/UmbracoPrism.Core/Controllers/MemberDashboardController.cs` for security issues in the proactive token warmup call. The review identified a missing try-catch wrapper around `await prismContext.GetAuthorizationHeaderAsync()`.

## Finding

**Severity:** Medium (Availability)

The warmup call lacks exception handling. While most failure paths in `GetAuthorizationHeaderAsync()` and `RefreshTokenAsync()` return `null` gracefully, unhandled exceptions from:
- `vault.GetSecretAsync()` (secret resolution failure)
- `IHttpClientFactory.CreateClient()` (factory initialization failure)  
- Other infrastructure exceptions outside Polly's `ShouldHandle` scope

...will propagate to the controller and result in a 500 error, preventing dashboard page load entirely.

## Remediation

Wrap warmup call in try-catch with warning-level logging. Fix applied by coordinator immediately after review completion.
